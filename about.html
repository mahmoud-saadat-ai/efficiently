About & Track Record

Built by operators

Mahmoud Saadat — Founder, Efficiently

Twenty years shipping Physical AI — from power ICs and energy-harvesting silicon, through robotaxi hardware, to co-founding an imaging radar company and leading SDV compute platforms. Efficiently is the advisory and investment practice built on that arc: system-level efficiency, product strategy, hiring, and fundraising for teams that have to work in the real world.

The Arc

Four eras. One through-line.

Physics, cost, and time-to-market bind at every layer — silicon, vehicle, radar, and fleet compute.

2002 – 2015

Analog & power IC design

Stanford PhD with Boris Murmann on energy-harvesting, power-aware sensing. Shipped boost converters and LED drivers at Intersil (e.g. ISL9111/9113) from definition through release — parts that ship in volume. Research converter: fully integrated switched-capacitor DC–DC for wireless sensor nodes.

  • Consumer, automotive, IoT sensors, medical implants
  • IEEE TCAS-I, A-SSCC, and related publications
2015 – 2019

Hardware for autonomous vehicles

Apple Special Projects — automotive DC–DC design and power-network simulation. Zoox — power distribution and contributions to proprietary sense and compute. Lyft Level 5 — Senior Hardware Engineering Manager for development, test, and validation.

  • HV / LV DC–DC converter ECUs
  • Vehicle power distribution
  • Sensor + compute + networking integration
2019 – 2024

Software-defined imaging radar

Co-founder, CEO, and Chief Architect of Zadar Labs — zero-to-one on high-resolution 4D imaging radar. Raised a $17M Series A, built a team of ~15, and shipped products deployed globally in robotics, automotive, industrial equipment, and critical infrastructure.

  • Multiple high-resolution imaging radar products
  • Radar and sensor-fusion perception software
  • Fleet datasets from a customized capture vehicle
2024 – present

Hardware for software-defined vehicles

Head of Hardware at Applied Intuition — grew the hardware org from early inception; owns strategy, roadmap, and execution for SDV compute across automotive, trucking, construction/mining, and related verticals. Platforms span central compute and infotainment, ADAS ECUs, zonal/body controllers, and I/O aggregators.

  • On-road and off-road SDV hardware
  • EE lab infrastructure and development process
  • Semiconductor and manufacturing partnerships
