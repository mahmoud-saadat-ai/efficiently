Hands-on guidance across the full Physical AI stack — sensing (imaging radar, LiDAR, cameras), embedded compute (MCUs, DSPs, FPGAs, GPUs), power distribution, high-speed interfaces (Automotive Ethernet, MIPI/CSI-2, CAN-FD), and validation automation.
