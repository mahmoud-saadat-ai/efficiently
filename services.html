Advisory

Strategic & Technical Advisory

Hands-on guidance across the full Physical AI stack — sensing (imaging radar, LiDAR, cameras), embedded compute (MCUs, DSPs, FPGAs, GPUs), power distribution, high-speed interfaces (Automotive Ethernet, MIPI/CSI-2, CAN-FD), and validation automation. Grounded in programs that left the lab: PMIC tapeouts, robotaxi fleets, radar deployments across continents, and SDV roadmaps for automotive, trucking, and heavy machinery OEMs.

  • Architecture & platform strategy
    Software-defined and zonal vehicle compute; centralized vs. distributed sensing; radar, LiDAR, and camera tradeoffs; platformization vs. one-off integration.
  • Hardware–software co-design
    Productive boundaries between ASIC/FPGA/MCU/GPU workloads, signal chain, data paths, and fleet validation — not just block diagrams.
  • Power, silicon, and BOM discipline
    Automotive power distribution, high-speed interfaces (Ethernet, MIPI/CSI-2, CAN-FD), semiconductor selection, and CapEx-aware integration.
  • Scale, fundraising, and team velocity
    Roadmaps that survive manufacturing and deployment; hiring and org design; investor narrative and diligence support from someone who raised a $17M Series A as CEO.

Engagement models: Retained advisory, project-based, or board-level.

Investment

Hands-On, Operator-Style

We invest selectively in early-stage Physical AI where domain experience in autonomy, radar, industrial sensing, or SDV compute can shorten the path to defensible product and credible traction.

What We Look For

  • Strong technical insight
    Founders who understand physics, safety, and cost constraints — not only models and demos.
  • Clear physical-world ambition
    Systems that sense, decide, and act in the field: robotics, mobility, industrial automation, infrastructure.
  • Fixable inefficiency
    Architecture, focus, or go-to-market gaps where operator judgment and network can change the trajectory in months, not years.

How We Add Value

  • Architecture and program reviews from someone who has owned outcomes from schematic to fleet and from seed deck to Series A
  • Product strategy, technical hiring, and org scaling patterns tested while building Zadar and scaling Applied hardware
  • Investor narrative, data-room readiness, and diligence support informed by multiple venture rounds as CEO
  • Introductions across automotive OEMs and Tier 1s, robotics and industrial end users, silicon vendors, and capital partners
  • Operator-style engagement — specific recommendations and follow-through, not passive capital

Stage: Pre-seed through Series A. Sector-focused, thesis-driven.
